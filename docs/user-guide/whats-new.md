## Batch 30: Text presets
- Save a text style once and reuse it: select a title, click **Save as preset** in the quick actions of **Edit Properties**, type a name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The preset keeps the whole look (font, size, colors, outline, shadow, background box, alignment and box width), not the words. See [Save a text style as a preset](text-presets.md#save-a-text-style-as-a-preset).
- The **Presets** tab of the Media panel now works. Each card draws its name in its own look. Point at a card to see it on the selected titles, click it to apply it (one undo step), or click it with no text selected to add a new title in that look at the playhead. Drag a card onto the timeline or the preview to add a text there. See [Apply a preset to text](text-presets.md#apply-a-preset-to-text).
- 12 starter presets come with the editor: **Title**, **Subtitle**, **Bold outline**, **Lower third**, **Caption bold**, **Highlight**, **Minimal mono**, **Big number**, **Elegant serif**, **Handwritten**, **Neon glow** and **Breaking**. See [Start with the starter presets](text-presets.md#start-with-the-starter-presets).
- **Update preset** in the quick actions writes a title's new style into its preset. Other titles that use the preset keep their style until you apply it to them again, as its tooltip says. See [Update a preset](text-presets.md#update-a-preset).
- Choose the presets <kbd>T</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> use: right-click a card and choose **Use as default title** or **Use as default subtitle**. See [Choose the presets for T and Shift+T](text-presets.md#choose-the-presets-for-t-and-shiftt).
- Mark a preset as a favorite, and a chip with its name appears in the timeline toolbar: one click adds a title in that look at the playhead (up to 8 chips). See [Put favorite presets in the timeline toolbar](text-presets.md#put-favorite-presets-in-the-timeline-toolbar).
- **Apply position** makes a preset place text too, for example a name in the lower left of the frame. See [Place text with a preset (Apply position)](text-presets.md#place-text-with-a-preset-apply-position).
- Keep presets for every project (**Global**) or inside one project's folder (**Project**, in `presets/text`). Search, rename, duplicate and delete them from the card menu, export and import them as JSON files, and keep your global presets in a library folder, for example on a synced drive. See [Keep presets for all projects or for one](text-presets.md#keep-presets-for-all-projects-or-for-one), [Export and import presets](text-presets.md#export-and-import-presets) and [Sync your presets with a folder](text-presets.md#sync-your-presets-with-a-folder).
- Presets look the same in projects of every size: their sizes follow the shorter side of the frame. The default subtitle of a landscape 1920×1080 project is now 900 pixels wide, as in a vertical one.
- New pages: [Text presets](text-presets.md). No new shortcuts: <kbd>T</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> now use the default presets; see [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md#text-presets).

## Batch 29: Titles and text
- Add a title with one key: press <kbd>T</kbd> (or click **Title** in the timeline toolbar), type, and press <kbd>Esc</kbd>. The title appears in the middle of the frame at the playhead, on the top video track or a new one above it, and lasts until the end of the video clip under the playhead (3 seconds when there is none). See [Add a title](titles-and-text.md#add-a-title).
- Add a subtitle with <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> (or **Subtitle**): it sits in the lower third with a dark box behind each line, wraps at a fixed width, and grows upwards as you add lines. See [Add a subtitle](titles-and-text.md#add-a-subtitle).
- With a text clip selected, <kbd>T</kbd> puts the new title 40 pixels below it, so stacked lines don't overlap.
- Type text right in the preview, at its real size and font. Double-click a text, or select it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, to edit it. The gold caret sits exactly between the letters at any zoom; select with the mouse or <kbd>Shift</kbd> and the arrow keys; <kbd>Enter</kbd> starts a new line; input methods for Asian languages work. <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> finishes, and the whole edit is one undo step. See [Edit text in the preview](titles-and-text.md#edit-text-in-the-preview).
- Change the font, the size or a color in **Edit Properties** while you type: the text updates and you keep typing where you were.
- A selected text has its own handles: the corners change the font size (outline, shadow and box grow with it) and the sides change the width of the text box, so the words wrap. See [Resize text with its handles](titles-and-text.md#resize-text-with-its-handles).
- The **Text** section of **Edit Properties**, built in the previous batch, is now yours: font, weight and italic, size, solid or gradient fill, outline, shadow, background box per line or for the whole block, alignment, anchor, letter spacing, line height, case, and an auto or fixed box. See [Style text in the Text section](titles-and-text.md#style-text-in-the-text-section).
- The font picker lists your favorites, the fonts used in the project, the eight fonts that come with the editor and, after **Show system fonts**, the fonts on your computer. A font from your computer is copied into the project's new `fonts` folder, so the project looks the same elsewhere. A font that is missing shows a red **Missing** chip and a red triangle on the clip. See [Choose a font](titles-and-text.md#choose-a-font) and [Fix a missing font](titles-and-text.md#fix-a-missing-font).
- A click on a text selects it only on its letters or its background box, so you can still click the picture behind the empty parts of a text.
- New pages: [Titles and text](titles-and-text.md). New shortcuts: <kbd>T</kbd>, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>, <kbd>Enter</kbd> and, while you edit text, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> and <kbd>Esc</kbd>; see [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md#text).

## Batch 28: Groundwork for text
- Nothing changes on screen in this version. This batch builds the text engine that titles, subtitles and captions use. Adding text to your project, with the dimmed **Title** and **Subtitle** buttons of the timeline toolbar, is available in a later version.
- The engine already has what those features need: text that stays sharp at every preview zoom, eight font families that come with the editor (Inter, Montserrat, Nunito, Oswald, Unbounded, Playfair Display, Caveat and JetBrains Mono), fonts installed on your computer, copied into the project folder so the project looks the same on another computer, and a warning when a project uses a font this computer doesn't have.

- Select clips with a click (a video and its linked sound together), <kbd>Shift</kbd>+click, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click (that clip alone) and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+click (only that clip), or drag a rectangle on empty track space to select every clip it touches. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> selects all clips and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> clears the selection.

- Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> to insert (later clips on every track shift right), <kbd>Alt</kbd> to duplicate, <kbd>Shift</kbd> to keep clips on their tracks, and press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. Drag above the top video track or below the bottom audio track to create a track.
- Nudge selected clips by 1 frame with <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Left</kbd>/<kbd>Right</kbd> (5 frames with <kbd>Shift</kbd>), and move them to the next track with <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Up</kbd>/<kbd>Down</kbd>.
- Every move is one undo step, and undo brings the selection back. The timeline scrolls when you hold the pointer near its edge while dragging clips, drawing a rectangle or scrubbing in the ruler.

See [Timeline](timeline.md) and [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md).

## Batch 10: Sound
- Playback now plays the sound of your audio tracks, in sync with the picture (less than one frame apart, with no drift), also for phone videos. Sound plays at 1x forward, in play around and across loops; jumps and pauses are free of clicks.
- **Mute** and a **Master volume** knob (-60.0 dB to 0.0 dB) in the transport bar set how loud you listen. They are remembered in this browser and do not change your project.
- A stereo audio meter along the right edge of the preview shows the peak level of your edit, from -60 to +6 dBFS, with a peak hold and clip lights that stay red until you click them.
- **M** (mute) and **S** (solo) toggles in each audio track header. Each click is one undo step and is heard at once, also while playing.
- After import, the sound of each audio file and video with sound is analyzed in the background and kept in the project's `cache` folder, ready for the waveforms of a later version. Long MP4, MOV and M4A sound loads in pieces as you play.
- Playback now starts a few hundredths of a second later than before after you press play, so that sound and picture start together.

See [Audio](audio.md), [Preview and playback](preview-and-playback.md) and [Timeline](timeline.md).

## Batch 09: Playback
- Play and pause with <kbd>Space</kbd>, shuttle with <kbd>J</kbd>, <kbd>K</kbd> and <kbd>L</kbd> up to 8x, hold <kbd>K</kbd> and tap <kbd>J</kbd> or <kbd>L</kbd> to step one frame, play around the playhead with <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, step with the arrow keys (hold to repeat), jump with <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd>, and loop with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>L</kbd>.
- A transport bar under the preview (**Go to start**, **Previous frame**, **Play**/**Pause**, **Next frame**, **Go to end**, **Loop playback**) with timecode, duration and frame-rate readouts, and a status strip that shows the frames **Dropped** in the last playback.
- During playback the timeline follows the playhead page by page, and clips that start within the next 2 seconds are prepared in advance so cuts play smoothly. Playback is silent for now.

See [Preview and playback](preview-and-playback.md) and [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md).

## Batch 08: Preview at the playhead
- The preview shows your edit at the playhead: every video and image clip there, stacked from track V1 upward.
- Clips fit the frame when you add them: clips with another aspect ratio get bands in the project's background color, clips with the same ratio fill the frame.
- Scrubbing in the ruler updates the preview live, with no black flashes at cuts.

See [Preview and playback](preview-and-playback.md).

## Batch 07: Timeline
- The timeline shows a ruler, track headers, and video and audio tracks with colored clips. Add tracks with **+ Video track** and **+ Audio track**.
- Zoom with the toolbar slider and buttons, with <kbd>=</kbd>, <kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>\\</kbd>, or with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+wheel at the pointer. Scroll with the wheel or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+wheel.
- Move the playhead by clicking or dragging in the ruler, with the arrow keys, <kbd>Home</kbd>, <kbd>End</kbd>, <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd>, or by typing a timecode such as `1:05:10` or `+30`.
- Double-click a media item to add it at the playhead: a video with sound becomes a video clip on V1 and its sound on A1, an image becomes a 5-second clip, and the playhead moves to the end so you can build a sequence quickly.

See [Timeline](timeline.md).

## Batch 06: Frame-accurate video
- Any frame of an imported video can be shown: the nearest available frame appears at once and the exact frame follows.
- Phone videos recorded upright are shown upright.
- Videos open faster the second time, and videos whose format your browser cannot decode are detected and reported.

See [Preview and playback](preview-and-playback.md) and [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Batch 05: Graphics-card preview
- The preview is drawn by your graphics card: the project frame sits centered at **Fit**, on the project background color.
- The preview header has a **Zoom** menu (Fit, 25% to 400%) and **Export frame**, which saves the current frame as a PNG at the project resolution.
- If your browser has no usable graphics adapter, the editor shows its unsupported-browser screen and says so. After a graphics driver reset, the preview reconnects and redraws on its own.

See [Preview and playback](preview-and-playback.md).

## Batch 04: Media import
- Import video (.mp4, .mov, .m4v), audio (.mp3, .m4a, .aac, .wav, .ogg) and images (.png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp, .gif, .svg) with **Import media** or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd>, by dropping files or whole folders on the Media panel, or by pasting images. Files are copied into the project folder.
- The Media panel shows a thumbnail, a duration badge and the details of each item, in a grid view or a list view with sortable columns. Select several items, rename with <kbd>F2</kbd> and remove with <kbd>Delete</kbd>.
- Large imports show their progress. Files that cannot be imported are named in a message, and the others are imported anyway.

## Batch 03: Projects, autosave and recovery
- A start screen with **New project** (9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 or a custom size; 23.976 to 60 fps), **Open project**, recent projects (up to 12), and opening a project by dropping its folder.
- Autosave one second after your last change, with a colored save dot and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>. The 20 newest backups are kept, an interrupted session offers to restore unsaved changes, and a folder you can only read opens in read-only mode with **Allow editing**.
- Rename the project in the app bar. The **Project** section of the Edit Properties panel sets the name, resolution, frame rate, background color and autosave delay, and each project remembers its view and panel sizes.

See [Getting started](getting-started.md) and [Projects and saving](projects-and-saving.md).

## Batch 02: Undo, redo and frame-exact time
- Undo and redo with the app bar buttons (the tooltip names the step), <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd>: up to 1,000 steps, and undo also restores the selection.
- Every position is a whole frame, and timecodes read minutes:seconds:frames. Timing stays exact at fractional frame rates such as 29.97 fps.

See [Projects and saving](projects-and-saving.md).
